Board games are social, and almost entirely visual. Our capstone team set out to build a device that reads the board for a player who can't see it, then hands that information back through touch and sound.

## How it works

- A camera watches the board, and a **real-time object detection pipeline built on YOLOv26n** tracks pieces and game state.
- The device translates that state into **tactile, audio, and haptic cues**, so a player knows where pieces are and what changed on the last turn.

## My role

I led the project end to end: scoping with the team, the vision pipeline, and integrating it with the feedback hardware so the whole loop ran in real time.
