Hospitals store MRI scans with inconsistent sequence labels, which makes building datasets slow and error-prone. An unsupervised clustering algorithm already worked for brain MRI; I explored whether it could do the same for the heart.

## What I did

- Adapted the clustering approach to cardiac MRI and its very different sequence types.
- Built the data preprocessing and ran the preliminary evaluations.

The work was accepted to the **Machine Learning for Clinical Decision Support (ML-CDS) Workshop at MICCAI 2025**.
